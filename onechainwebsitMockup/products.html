The digital will lifecycle.

WillsLedger is still being built. Here is how the Hong Kong–focused lifecycle is designed to work when it launches.

Step 01

Guided Will preparation

Walk the testator through appointing executors, naming beneficiaries, the residuary estate, optional gifts or guardianship, and flags that may need professional legal review. AI can assist intake and guidance; the Will wording itself is produced through controlled clause logic — not unrestricted AI drafting.

Who acts
Testator (guided)
AI role
Intake & guidance only
Result
Structured Will draft
Step 02

Paper signing & iAM Smart checks

The Will remains a physically signed paper document and must follow Hong Kong execution requirements. iAM Smart is intended to verify the identities of the testator, witnesses, and authorised executor at the relevant stages — digital checks do not replace legal signing and witnessing.

Who acts
Testator & witnesses
Identity
iAM Smart
Legal form
Signed paper Will
Step 03

Hash, confirmations & audit refs

After signing, WillsLedger can generate a cryptographic hash of the exact Will document and record related witness confirmations, timestamps, version information, and audit references. The Will and personal data stay off-chain; the ledger retains hashes and verification references so integrity can be checked later.

On-chain
Hash & evidence refs
Off-chain
Will & personal data
Result
Integrity checkable
Step 04

Executor handover access

After the testator’s death and satisfaction of the relevant access conditions, an authorised executor can verify their identity and open a limited, read-only record — the Will record, document hash, witness-confirmation trail, and audit information — without claiming the digital record itself creates or validates the Will.

Access

Result
Verified handover trail
