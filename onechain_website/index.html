HOW IT WORKS

From data to verified proof in three steps

OneChain anchors enterprise data on a permissioned Hyperledger Fabric chain — so partners, regulators, and end users can verify without crypto complexity.

01

Collect & sign

Enterprise apps, IoT sensors, and RVMs submit signed data through OneChain APIs or consortium channels.

02

Anchor on-chain

Data is hashed and recorded on the OneChain Consortium with PoA consensus — immutable, auditable, zero gas fees.

03

Verify anywhere

Anyone can verify credentials, credits, or records via QR scan, API call, or dashboard — no wallet required.

Verification without compromise

OneChain Consortium is secured by Hyperledger Fabric with Proof-of-Authority consensus, enabling enterprise data and credentials to be freely composable across platforms and applications without compromising privacy or auditability.
