What We Solve

Broken trust is expensive. We fix it at the root.

Every row below is a real problem our customers had before OneChain — and how the platform solves it.

The Problem

Certificates are trivially faked

Paper diplomas, PDF licences, and training records can be forged in minutes. Verifying one takes phone calls and weeks.

Our Answer

CertLedger

Credentials anchored on-chain at issuance. Anyone verifies authenticity in seconds with a QR scan — no account, no phone call.

The Problem

ESG reporting runs on trust-me data

Sustainability claims are self-reported and unauditable. Greenwashing erodes investor confidence and regulator patience.

Our Answer

ESGLedger

Impact data captured at source, verified by AI, and anchored immutably. Compliance reports generated from provable facts.

The Problem

Enterprise blockchain is too hard to adopt

Most chains are built for crypto, not compliance. Integration takes years and regulatory sign-off never comes.

Our Answer

OneChain Platform

A permissioned, energy-efficient stack designed alongside regulators — with APIs that plug into existing systems in weeks, not years.
