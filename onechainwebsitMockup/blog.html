OneChain News

Powering GBA Halal Certification Center on-chain

The Halal industry in Hong Kong is growing rapidly, and we're excited to share how OneChain is contributing to this important movement. Through CertLedger, we're empowering trusted organizations like the GBA Halal Certification Center (GHCC) to issue blockchain-based certificates — offering immutable proof of authenticity and validity. GHCC plays a critical role in ensuring all processes adhere to Shariah principles and Halal standards, building trust and credibility in the Hong Kong Halal industry. Proud to be part of an effort driving transparency, innovation, and growth in this dynamic and globally important sector.
