How It Works

Three steps to blockchain-verified credentials.

From issuance to verification — the entire flow is on-chain and instant.

1

Issue

Organisation creates a blockchain credential via the platform or API. Custom templates, batch issuance, and branding supported.

2

Hold

Recipient stores their verifiable certificate. Share instantly via QR code or secure link — anytime, anywhere.

3

Verify

Anyone can authenticate instantly. Scan the QR or enter the certificate ID — blockchain confirms validity in seconds.
