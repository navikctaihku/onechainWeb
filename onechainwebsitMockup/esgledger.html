Credit Lifecycle

From collection to retirement — fully verified.

Every plastic credit passes through five immutable stages on-chain. No double-counting. No greenwashing.

1

Collect

Plastic waste gathered by recyclers, logged with photos, GPS, and weight evidence.

2

Verify

Third-party verifiers confirm data and approve or reject each submission.

3

Mint

Verified credits tokenised on blockchain as tradable digital assets.

4

Trade

Buyers purchase verified credits on the ESGLedger marketplace.

5

Retire

Credits permanently retired after use. Immutable proof of impact.
