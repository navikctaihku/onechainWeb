Join us at the PoC Programme Innovation Showcase

Hong Kong is a dense, international city, and making circular recycling widespread, transparent and efficient takes innovative, forward-looking solutions. Come and see how OneChain, an award-winning team in the Proof-of-Concept (PoC) Programme, is doing it. Using CSDI spatial data and geospatial intelligence, OneChain combined foot-traffic analysis, community environments and gaps in recycling-network coverage to plan the best placement of collection points and smart recycling machines, so sites are chosen more precisely and deliver more value. Just as importantly, OneChain links physical recycling to a digital reporting framework, turning raw spatial data into traceable, verifiable data assets. This raises the transparency and credibility of recycling data and unlocks its wider value in support of the circular economy. Meet OneChain at the Innovation Showcase, try our solution in person, and exchange ideas with professionals from many sectors on using spatial data to create commercial and social value for smart-city and sustainable development.
