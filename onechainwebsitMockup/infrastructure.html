Permissioned by Design

The advantages only a permissioned chain can deliver.

Public chains were built for anonymous strangers. OneChain is built for regulated businesses — and that changes everything.

Data privacy by default

Private data channels keep commercial information between authorised parties only. Competitors never see your transactions — regulators can, when you grant access.

Every participant is known

No anonymous actors. Every node is a vetted, accountable organisation with a real-world identity — the foundation for legal enforceability and trust.

Zero gas fees, predictable cost

No volatile tokens, no bidding for block space. Transaction costs are $0 and operating costs are a flat, budgetable subscription.

Compliance-ready governance

Role-based access, audit trails, and data-residency control map directly onto GDPR, PDPO, and industry regulations — no legal workarounds needed.

Enterprise performance

Because validators are known, consensus is fast. 1000+ TPS with sub-second finality — no waiting on global miner lotteries.

Green by architecture

Proof-of-Authority consensus uses a fraction of the energy of mining — an ESG story your blockchain infrastructure supports instead of undermines.
