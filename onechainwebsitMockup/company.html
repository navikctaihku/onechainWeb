What is a verifiable credential?

A verifiable credential is a digital certificate — a degree, a licence, a compliance attestation — whose fingerprint is anchored on a blockchain the moment it's issued. Because that record can never be altered, anyone can confirm the certificate is genuine in seconds by scanning a QR code: no phone calls, no emails, no accounts. A forged or edited document simply won't match its on-chain fingerprint. This is the foundation CertLedger is built on, and one of the core things we help organisations put on-chain.
